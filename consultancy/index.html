The problem

Most AI projects start with the tool. They should start with the work.

Teams buy a tool, run a pilot, and nothing sticks, because nobody wrote down how the work really happens. AI also makes things up. Without a clear picture of the process, you cannot tell which steps are safe to automate.

Three ways to work together

Start small, go as deep as the work needs.

01

Discovery Call

90 minutes

You tell me about your AI initiatives, what is running, what stalled, what you are considering. You leave with an opportunity map and clear next steps, and you can ask me anything along the way.

02

AI Workflow Audit

1 day of interviews in person, 2 days remote · map delivered within a week

I spend a day inside your company interviewing the people who do the work (two days if we work remotely). You get a full process map of how work actually happens, with concrete suggestions for what to automate and what to leave with a person.

03

AI Adoption Workshop

Half day or full day · scoped per engagement

A hands-on session for non-technical teams, built around your own processes and tools. People leave knowing where AI helps their work, where it does not, and how to use it with a person always checking the result.
