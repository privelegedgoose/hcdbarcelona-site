The podcast

HCD Barcelona

A monthly video podcast of long, unhurried interviews about how work, technology, cities and belonging are changing, with Barcelona as the place we observe from. Formerly Habla con Daria.

Get the newsletter Watch on YouTube

The format

Long conversations, new episodes monthly.

Interviews run 45 to 60 minutes and are published on the first Thursday of the month. AI-themed episodes are recorded live in front of an audience.

Languages

Heard in one language, readable in four.

Each episode is recorded in whichever language gives the best interview: English, Spanish or Catalan. Every episode is subtitled in English, Spanish, Catalan and Russian.

Why it exists

About a third of Barcelona's residents were born abroad, yet Spanish-language media usually treat them as a topic of debate, not as people with a voice. This show turns that around.
