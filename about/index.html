About

Daria Gavrilova

AI adoption and enablement specialist, journalist and host of HCD Barcelona.

One career, not four.

I spent more than ten years as a journalist, at Kommersant, Afisha, Diari ARA and La Maleta de Portbou. I taught for around seven years, at Geneva Business School and UAB, and designed curricula. Now I work on AI adoption.

The thread is the same each time: take a complex field, turn it into a clear narrative, and guide someone through it.

How I think about AI.

It hallucinates, so the work is knowing where to use it and where not. You cannot know that until you understand how an organisation runs without AI. A human stays in the loop, always.

HCD Barcelona

A podcast, and a brand for the change I study.

HCD Barcelona began as Habla con Daria. It is now a video podcast about how work, technology, cities and belonging are changing, and the home of my consultancy.
