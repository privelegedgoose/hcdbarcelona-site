Consultancy

AI adoption for teams that are not technical.

For content, education, marketing and communications teams who want to use AI well: knowing where it saves real time, where it creates risk, and where a person must stay in charge.

Book a free process conversation

The problem

Most AI projects start with the tool. They should start with the work.

Teams buy a tool, run a pilot, and nothing sticks, because nobody wrote down how the work really happens. AI also makes things up. Without a clear picture of the process, you cannot tell which steps are safe to automate.

How it works

Three steps, with a free first pass.

01

Tell me how you work

A 60 to 90 minute guided conversation where you describe your processes in your own words. No preparation needed. This step is free.

02

Get a process map

I turn the conversation into a clear process map and a short list of automation suggestions. AI helps me draft it; I review it for accuracy and add my own judgement. It is sent to you at no cost.

03

Build what deserves it

If something is worth automating, we scope it together and I build it, with a person reviewing the output. Scope and price are agreed before any paid work.

Principles

How I work

  • No 100% automation
  • Simplest thing that works
  • GDPR-aware by default
