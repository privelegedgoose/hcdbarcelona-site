AI adoption consultancy and independent media · Barcelona

HCD Barcelona helps organisations use AI where it works, and only there.

HCD Barcelona is a Barcelona-based company with two practices: an AI adoption consultancy for non-technical teams, and an independent video podcast about how work, technology, cities and belonging are changing. Both begin the same way, by looking closely at how things really run before anyone says what should change.

The company's position

AI hallucinates, so the skill is knowing where to use it and where not. That judgement depends on understanding how an organisation runs without AI first, and HCD Barcelona keeps a person in the loop at every stage.

Consultancy

Map the process, then decide what deserves automation.

HCD Barcelona documents how a team actually works and identifies which steps are worth automating and which should stay with a person, backed by workshops that bring the whole team along.

About the podcast

Founder

Founded by Daria Gavrilova.

Daria Gavrilova is an AI adoption and enablement specialist, journalist and host. Her careers in journalism, teaching and curriculum design shaped the company's method: take a complex field, turn it into a clear narrative, and guide people through it.
