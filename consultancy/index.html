A model moved into the browser when the planned hosting needed a paid tier

A Barcelona recycling-sorting app

Original343 MB
Quantised87 MB

A fine-tuned Vision Transformer sorts a photo of an item into the right Barcelona recycling bin, with local edge cases built in and an honest "not sure" answer whenever confidence drops below 55%. When the planned hosting route turned out to need a paid tier, the model was exported to ONNX, quantised and moved entirely into the browser: no server, no hosting cost, and photos never leave the device. A side-by-side check on a test image showed the confidence within about 0.2 percentage points of the original. Tests on deliberately messy photos exposed a limitation, which was recorded rather than hidden. Try the app
